2. **Lead Cloud-Native Architecture & Event Streaming (Months 4–8):**

3. **Formalize Systems Architecture & Technical Governance (Months 9–12):**
* Lead system design reviews, author Architecture Decision Records (ADRs), and formalize CI/CD pipeline automation standards.
* Serve as technical mentor to intermediate and junior developers, bridging system specifications with business stakeholders and engineering pods.

* **Broad vs. Deep Positioning:** The background lists a wide array of legacy/hybrid skills (Perl, VB-style scripting references, NETCONF, Angular, Java, C++), which risks reading like a generalist maintainer rather than a focused, modern backend/distributed systems authority.

* **Feasibility Verdict:** High feasibility for Senior/Lead Backend and Infrastructure Automation roles; requires explicit framing around cloud scalability and microservices to clear staff/principal-level engineering bars.
