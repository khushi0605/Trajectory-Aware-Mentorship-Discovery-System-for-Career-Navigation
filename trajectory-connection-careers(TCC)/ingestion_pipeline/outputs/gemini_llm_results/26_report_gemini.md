# Baseline Trajectory Report

**Background:** Allen Chaudhari is a seasoned web designer with over 6 years of experience, specializing in UI/UX design, custom graphics creation, and mobile design, and has received recognition such as the AWWWARDS prize.
**Goal:** Allen aims to continue advancing in web design, focusing on enhancing user experience and client satisfaction in a dynamic and fast-paced environment.

## 1. Recommended Path

* **Phase 1: Modernize Tooling & Artifact Shift (Months 1–3)**
* Transition core design workflow entirely to Figma. Rebuild the personal portfolio (allentchaudhari.com) using a modern static generator or component framework (e.g., Next.js, Webflow, or Framer).
* Structure case studies around measurable product outcomes rather than purely visual deliverables: define the user problem, research methodology, wireframes, usability test results, and final business impact metrics (e.g., retention, conversion).


* **Phase 2: Target Senior UI/UX Designer / Product Designer Roles (Months 4–6)**
* Target fast-paced tech startups, digital product consultancies, or established SaaS companies looking for versatile designers with front-end technical fluency.
* Position past experience—especially the 35% UX improvement, 18% retention lift, and AWWWARDS win—as evidence of bridging high visual craft with concrete business metrics.


* **Phase 3: Advance to Lead Product Designer or Design Systems Lead (Years 2–3)**
* Own multi-platform design systems and scale client or product user experiences across complex web applications.
* Drive user research programs, mentor junior designers, and directly manage client or executive stakeholder strategy.



**Reasoning:**

Allen already possesses a proven track record of agency speed, award-winning visual execution, and client satisfaction. However, the industry has shifted away from traditional monolithic "Web Designer" roles toward specialized **Product Designer** and **Senior UX/UI Designer** positions. By reframing past agency velocity and quantitative wins into modern product design artifacts, Allen can step directly into senior-level roles in agile, fast-paced environments.

## 2. Risk Analysis & Feasibility Flags

* **Legacy Tooling & Methodology Gap:** Allen's documented toolset centers on Adobe Creative Suite, jQuery, PHP, and MySQL. Modern dynamic environments predominantly rely on Figma, design token workflows, and component-based frontend collaboration (React/Vue).
* **Role Taxonomy Risk ("Web Designer" vs. "Product/UX Designer"):** The title "Web Designer" is frequently perceived by recruiters as marketing- or visual-focused rather than system-driven. Without explicit positioning around iterative user testing, information architecture, and accessibility standards, Allen risks being screened out of high-impact UX roles.
* **Resume Timeline Recency:** The chronological work history cuts off around 2017. Gaps or unlisted recent work present a significant vetting risk; Allen must ensure recent freelance, contract, or full-time engagements are clearly represented.
* **Market Competition:** High competition in mid-to-senior UX roles requires candidates to present deeply detailed case studies rather than visual mockup galleries.

## 3. Experience Analysis & Skills to Acquire

**Technical Skills to Acquire:**

* **Design & Prototyping Systems:** Industry-standard proficiency in **Figma** (Auto Layout, Variants, Variables, Component Properties, and interactive prototyping).
* **Modern Design Systems:** Building and documenting multi-brand design tokens, UI kits, and component libraries aligned with design-to-code workflows (e.g., Storybook parity).
* **Accessibility (a11y):** WCAG 2.1/2.2 AA compliance standards, color contrast analysis, keyboard navigation, and screen reader flow considerations.
* **User Research & Validation:** Quantitative and qualitative methodologies, including unmoderated usability testing (e.g., Maze, UserTesting), card sorting, tree testing, and customer journey mapping.
* **Modern Frontend Understanding:** Fundamental understanding of component architectures (React, Tailwind CSS) to interface seamlessly with modern product engineering teams.

**Common Transition Hurdles:**

* **Letting Go of "Pixel-First" Habits:** Moving from delivering static page layouts to designing dynamic, responsive application states (empty states, edge cases, error handlings, loading behaviors).
* **Navigating Product Discovery vs. Delivery:** Agency web designers are accustomed to fast, unidirectional delivery. Product UX requires iterative discovery, stakeholder alignment, and continuous experimentation rather than one-off launches.
* **Balancing Aesthetics with Usability:** Navigating trade-offs where visually flashy designs must yield to conversion optimization, performance, and accessibility constraints.

## 4. Mentor Discovery Strategy

Allen should target mentors with the following backgrounds:

* **Target Profiles & Job Titles:**
* **Lead Product Designer / Staff Product Designer** at a scaling B2B or B2C SaaS company who previously worked in design agencies.
* **Design Director / VP of Design** at a modern digital agency (e.g., Work & Co, Fantasy, Metalab) that focuses on product design and digital transformation.
* **Design Systems Lead** with a background in hybrid design-engineering (former web designers or creative technologists).


* **Strategic Value of These Mentors:**
* **Translation of Experience:** A mentor who transitioned from traditional web design/agency environments to modern product design can provide tactical feedback on reframing legacy case studies into product-led stories.
* **Portfolio Reviews:** High-level critique on Figma component hygiene, user-centered case study structure, and demonstrating ROI to product hiring managers.
* **Navigating Cross-Functional Collaboration:** Guidance on interfacing effectively with modern Product Managers (PMs) and Engineering teams in fast-paced sprint cadences.
