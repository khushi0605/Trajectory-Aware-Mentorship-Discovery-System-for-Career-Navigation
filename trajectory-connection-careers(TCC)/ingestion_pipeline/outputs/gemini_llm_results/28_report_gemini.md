# Baseline Trajectory Report

**Background:** Paul Satchell is a seasoned Python developer with extensive experience in data solutions, database engineering, and cloud-based technologies, holding certifications in Agile, Cloudera, and Python.

**Goal:** Paul aims to continue advancing in his career as a senior Python developer, focusing on leading teams and delivering high-performance data solutions.

## 1. Recommended Path

* **Phase 1: Position for Technical Leadership (Months 1–4)**
* Formalize hybrid responsibilities into a unified Staff Data Engineer or Lead Backend/Data Engineer narrative, highlighting team leadership (10-person Scrum team, mentoring 5+ engineers) alongside backend optimization (slashing request latencies from 700ms to 150ms and lifting DB performance by 20%).
* Modernize legacy big data patterns: supplement MapReduce/Hadoop foundations with modern lakehouse patterns (Delta Lake/Iceberg) and distributed orchestration (Airflow, Dagster).


* **Phase 2: Transition into Lead / Principal Data Engineer or Engineering Manager (Months 5–12)**
* Target tech-forward mid-market to enterprise companies scaling mission-critical data platforms, high-throughput backend services, or streaming architectures.
* Lead architectural design reviews, establish organization-wide data governance, and drive performance-tuning initiatives across multi-node cloud environments.


* **Phase 3: Advance to Director of Data Engineering or Head of Data Platform (Years 2–4)**
* Expand influence beyond single delivery teams to oversee broader platform strategy, data infrastructure budgets, and multi-team roadmaps across engineering, data science, and business analytics.



**Reasoning:**

Paul has over 15 years of industry experience and has already mastered senior-level development, Scrum master responsibilities, performance tuning, and technical mentoring. Staying purely at the "Senior Python Developer" title underutilizes his extensive leadership and data engineering capabilities. Moving decisively into **Lead Data Engineer**, **Staff Backend Engineer**, or **Engineering Manager** matches his proven track record of delivering end-to-end data systems and coordinating cross-functional teams.

## 2. Risk Analysis & Feasibility Flags

* **Legacy Big Data & Infrastructure Stack:** Paul's certifications and tooling highlight older enterprise stacks (Hadoop, Cloudera CCA from 2011, Docker Swarm, Puppet, SVN). Modern high-performance data platforms heavily expect Kubernetes, cloud-native managed services (AWS EMR/Glue, GCP BigQuery, Snowflake), and modern pipeline orchestrators.
* **Title Compression & Career Stagnation Perception:** With a B.S. earned in 2006, holding a generic "Senior Python Developer" title since 2018 may give hiring committees the false impression of career plateauing unless framed explicitly as a tech lead and architecture driver.
* **Outdated Certifications:** The credentials listed (PCPP2 2009, Cloudera 2011, PMI-ACP 2014) are dated. While foundational knowledge is solid, modern hiring teams look for recency in production architectures and cloud systems.
* **Underemphasized Real-Time Streaming:** The resume leans heavily on batch systems (Hadoop, SQL materialized views, Django ORM). Modern high-performance data ecosystems heavily favor event-driven streaming (Kafka, Flink, Spark Streaming).

## 3. Experience Analysis & Skills to Acquire

**Technical Skills to Acquire:**

* **Modern Cloud Data Platforms:** Deep production expertise in Snowflake, Databricks, BigQuery, or AWS Redshift/Athena.
* **Stream Processing & Message Brokers:** Apache Kafka, Redpanda, or Apache Flink for real-time distributed data pipelines.
* **Modern Orchestration & DataOps:** Apache Airflow, Prefect, or Dagster, coupled with data modeling tools like dbt (data build tool).
* **Container Orchestration & Cloud Native CI/CD:** Kubernetes (EKS/GKE), Helm, and modern CI/CD (GitHub Actions/GitLab CI) replacing Puppet, SVN, and Docker Swarm.
* **Distributed Query Optimization:** Advanced execution plan profiling in PySpark and distributed vector/columnar storage engines (Parquet, Arrow, Polars).

**Common Transition Hurdles:**

* **Shifting from Implementation to System Architecture:** Transitioning from writing Python scripts and stored procedures to designing resilient distributed architectures, failure modes, and schema evolutions.
* **Unlearning Legacy Big Data Overhead:** Moving away from managing traditional on-prem Hadoop clusters and manual map-reduce semantics to leveraging serverless, decoupled compute-and-storage platforms.
* **Balancing People Management vs. Technical Depth:** Defining whether the primary growth track is technical architecture (Staff/Principal Engineer) or organizational/people leadership (Engineering Manager).

## 4. Mentor Discovery Strategy

Paul should target mentors with the following backgrounds:

* **Target Profiles & Job Titles:**
* **Director of Data Platform / VP of Data Engineering** who grew out of backend Python/data engineering roots and manages scalable data systems.
* **Principal / Staff Data Engineer** at a hyper-growth tech company or high-traffic consumer platform who has led migrations from legacy batch/Hadoop systems to modern lakehouses and streaming systems.
* **Engineering Manager (Data Infrastructure)** who made the deliberate jump from Senior Developer/Scrum Master to people leadership in data-intensive organizations.


* **Strategic Value of These Mentors:**
* **Career Path Calibration:** Helping Paul resolve the fork between the Staff-plus individual contributor track and engineering management based on his dual development/Scrum master background.
* **Stack Modernization Strategy:** Advising on how to showcase legacy data wins while framing them in terms of modern cloud-native architectural patterns.
* **Executive & Organizational Communication:** Coaching Paul on shifting communication from sprint deliverables and unit testing to business impact, platform ROI, and engineering culture.
