# Baseline Trajectory Report

**Background:** Maudie Leffler is a data scientist with expertise in predictive modeling, machine learning, and data visualization, possessing a strong background in computer science and experience in managing research teams and publishing analytical reports.

**Goal:** Aspiring to continue advancing in data science by leveraging predictive analytics and machine learning to drive strategic business decisions and operational improvements.

## 1. Recommended Path

* **Phase 1: Quantify Business Impact & Modernize MLOps Narratives (Months 1–3)**
* Reframe historical accomplishments around bottom-line business ROI (e.g., revenue generated, churn prevented, cost savings, or latency reductions) rather than generic job responsibilities.
* Update portfolio and interview case studies with modern production machine learning lifecycles: automated retraining pipelines, feature stores, experiment tracking, and model governance.


* **Phase 2: Target Lead / Staff Data Scientist or Analytics Manager Roles (Months 4–8)**
* Target mature tech enterprises, fintech platforms, or high-growth B2B/B2C SaaS firms looking for seasoned individual contributors capable of bridging executive business strategy and deep ML modeling.
* Lead strategic cross-functional initiatives pairing predictive classifiers with growth marketing, customer lifecycle modeling, and supply chain/operational forecasting.


* **Phase 3: Transition to Head of Data Science or Director of Decision Science (Years 2–4)**
* Take ownership of the data science roadmap, staffing, and compute budgets across multiple business verticals.
* Establish enterprise-wide standards for experimental design (A/B testing, causal inference), predictive modeling governance, and algorithmic fairness.



**Reasoning:**

With over a decade of continuous industry experience spanning technical model development, team supervision, and executive presentation, Maudie has outgrown standard mid-level Data Scientist roles. Given the stated goal of using predictive analytics to drive high-level strategic decisions and operations, the natural trajectory is to step into **Lead/Staff Data Scientist** or **Director of Decision Science / Analytics**, where business strategy and statistical modeling directly intersect.

## 2. Risk Analysis & Feasibility Flags

* **Generic Resume Phrasing & Boilerplate Risk:** The current resume relies heavily on passive job description language ("Building and productionizing predictive models...", "Pursuing a BS/BA/MS...") rather than specific, quantified accomplishments. Hiring managers at top-tier firms may view this as a lack of tangible ownership.
* **Missing Tooling Specifics:** Crucial modern technical frameworks are omitted from the profile text (e.g., specific Python/R libraries like PyTorch, scikit-learn, XGBoost; modern cloud platforms like AWS, GCP, or Azure; workflow orchestrators like Airflow/Kubeflow).
* **Legacy Analytics Tooling Signals:** Prominently listing SPSS and Excel alongside Tableau signals legacy business intelligence rather than modern scalable data science and MLOps.
* **Ambiguity in Degree Timeline:** Stating "Pursuing a BS/BA/MS..." under employment bullet points while listing a completed UCSD B.S. in Computer Science creates confusing educational and timeline signals for recruiters.

## 3. Experience Analysis & Skills to Acquire

**Technical Skills to Acquire:**

* **MLOps & Deployment Ecosystem:** Model deployment frameworks (FastAPI, Docker, Triton/TorchServe), orchestration tools (Airflow, Prefect, Kubeflow), and experiment tracking (MLflow, Weights & Biases).
* **Causal Inference & Advanced Experimentation:** Beyond basic classification/regression: uplift modeling, difference-in-differences, synthetic controls, and multi-armed bandits to directly measure operational and growth intervention effects.
* **Modern Distributed Data Processing:** PySpark, Polars, and distributed SQL query engines across modern lakehouses (Databricks, Snowflake, BigQuery).
* **Feature Engineering Platforms:** Familiarity with modern feature management tools (e.g., Feast, Hopsworks) and automated data quality assertions (Great Expectations).

**Common Transition Hurdles:**

* **Shifting from Model Accuracy to Business Utility:** Overcoming the instinct to focus purely on algorithmic performance metrics (AUC-ROC, RMSE) rather than downstream operational metrics (customer acquisition cost, retention lift, compute cost efficiency).
* **Navigating the Data Science vs. ML Engineering Split:** Deciding whether to lean heavily into backend engineering (ML infrastructure/deployment) or toward product and strategy (decision intelligence, causal modeling, executive storytelling).
* **Aligning Cross-Functional Stakeholders:** Translating complex non-linear models and probabilistic forecasts into intuitive, actionable levers for non-technical executives and product managers.

## 4. Mentor Discovery Strategy

Maudie should target mentors with the following backgrounds:

* **Target Profiles & Job Titles:**
* **Director / VP of Data Science & Decision Intelligence** at an enterprise marketplace, fintech, or digital subscription company.
* **Staff / Principal Data Scientist (Product & Growth)** who transitioned from traditional statistical data science into high-level business strategy.
* **Head of Analytics & Data Science** who manages blended teams of data scientists, machine learning engineers, and operational analysts.


* **Strategic Value of These Mentors:**
* **Executive Framing:** Mentorship on translating complex model architectures into board-level strategic roadmaps and business-case justifications.
* **Resume & Case Study Modernization:** Tactical guidance on replacing boilerplate bullet points with high-impact, metrics-driven case studies that stand out in Staff/Lead-level interviews.
* **Organizational Influence:** Guidance on establishing buy-in for predictive operational models across resistant operational and business leadership teams.
