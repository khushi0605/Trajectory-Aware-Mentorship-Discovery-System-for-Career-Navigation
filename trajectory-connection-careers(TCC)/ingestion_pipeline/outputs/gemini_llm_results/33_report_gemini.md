2. **Expand into Advanced Modeling & Foundation Architectures (Months 4–8):**
* Integrate modern deep learning architectures (Transformers, PyTorch, Hugging Face) and advanced causal inference alongside traditional scikit-learn/gradient-boosting pipelines.
* Lead high-impact cross-functional initiatives connecting machine learning predictions directly with product personalization loops and automated enterprise decision-making engines.


3. **Drive Organizational AI Strategy & Mentorship (Months 9–12):**
* Establish algorithmic governance frameworks, model risk management guidelines, and A/B testing statistical rigor across all business analytics units.
* Mentor senior and mid-level data scientists, author enterprise AI technical roadmaps, and present AI strategy directly to executive leadership.
