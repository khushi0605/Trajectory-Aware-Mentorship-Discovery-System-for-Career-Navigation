# Baseline Trajectory Report

**Background:** Karen Philips is a dynamic Web Designer with 3 years of experience in creating and maintaining responsive websites for travel companies, proficient in WordPress, Drupal, HTML5, CSS, and Adobe Creative Suite.
**Goal:** Karen aims to continue her career as a Web Designer, focusing on enhancing user experience and customer retention through innovative design and technical expertise.

---

## 1. Recommended Path

* **Phase 1: Modernize Design & Prototyping Toolchain (Months 1–6)**
* **Objective:** Transition from legacy design software (Sketch, static Photoshop/Illustrator mockups) to modern collaborative product design standards.
* **Focus Areas:** Master Figma (auto-layout, components, interactive prototyping, variables, tokens), design system fundamentals, and responsive web component libraries (Tailwind CSS, modern semantic HTML/CSS).
* **Milestone:** Build and publish a comprehensive, interactive travel booking web design system in Figma, complete with responsive mobile/desktop variants and documented design tokens.


* **Phase 2: Transition from CMS Web Design to UX/Product Design (Months 6–18)**
* **Objective:** Expand from visual asset creation and CMS page assembly (WordPress, Drupal) into end-to-end UX/UI and Product Design methodologies.
* **Focus Areas:** User research methods (usability testing, user journey mapping, heatmaps via Hotjar/FullStory), conversion rate optimization (A/B testing via Optimizely/VWO), accessibility compliance (WCAG 2.2 AA), and modern frontend fundamentals (modern JavaScript ES6+, headless CMS like Contentful/Sanity).
* **Milestone:** Deliver an end-to-end UX redesign case study for a high-traffic travel or e-commerce booking funnel, documenting quantitative retention and conversion improvements from research to handoff.


* **Phase 3: Mature into Senior Web / Product Designer (Year 2+)**
* **Objective:** Establish ownership over product design initiatives driving customer retention and user engagement.
* **Focus Areas:** Cross-functional handoff with frontend engineering teams, design sprint facilitation, UX metrics tracking (HEART framework, System Usability Scale), and data-driven product iteration.
* **Milestone:** Secure a Senior Web / Product Designer role at a travel tech, consumer tech, or digital agency, owning user journeys that directly improve conversion and customer retention metrics.



**Reasoning:** The candidate has strong historical brand pedigree in the travel vertical (Expedia Group, FarePortal, The Points Guy) and a demonstrated track record of improving metrics (e.g., raising UX scores by 38%, improving retention by 17%). However, the traditional "Web Designer" title spanning graphic design, marketing email production, and legacy CMS manipulation (WordPress, Joomla, Drupal) has largely bifurcated into either **Product/UX Designer** or **Frontend UI Engineer**. Pivoting toward modern UX/UI and Product Design leverages her core strengths in wireframing, user funnels, and retention without requiring a pivot into heavy full-stack programming.

---

## 2. Risk Analysis & Feasibility Flags

* **Significant Employment Gap:** The documented experience ends in May 2018 at Expedia Group. A multi-year gap without recorded commercial experience will trigger screening filters unless accounted for through freelance work, agency contracts, continuing education, or personal portfolio initiatives.
* **Dated Toolchain & Industry Terminology:** The resume highlights older CMS tools (Joomla, Drupal) and design workflows (Sketch, Photoshop for web design). Contemporary design teams operate predominantly on Figma, collaborative design systems, and headless architectures.
* **Blur Between Marketing Design and Product UX:** The experience spans email marketing templates, promotional banners, and graphic assets alongside wireframing and user funnels. Top-tier design teams look for clear specialization—either in conversion-driven visual/growth design or interaction-heavy product/UX design.
* **Template & Presentation Artifacts:** The resume contains placeholder contact details (`email@email.com`), non-standard date formats (`1995/20/03`), and unnecessary personal fields (driving license, date of birth, nationality). In modern design hiring, visual presentation, typography, and portfolio links are under intense scrutiny; an unpolished resume format directly undermines a designer's candidacy.

---

## 3. Experience Analysis & Skills to Acquire

**Technical Skills to Acquire:**

* **Industry-Standard Design & Prototyping:** Advanced Figma (component variants, interactive components, autolayout, responsive constraints, variables, and prototyping plugins).
* **Design Systems & Token Architecture:** Creating and maintaining scalable design systems, understanding token architecture, and aligning with frontend frameworks like Tailwind CSS.
* **UX Research & Quantitative Analytics:** User testing platforms (UserTesting, Maze), quantitative analytics (Google Analytics 4, Mixpanel), session recordings, and structured A/B testing methodologies.
* **Web Accessibility (a11y):** Designing for WCAG 2.2 standards, color contrast compliance, keyboard navigation hierarchy, and screen reader-friendly layouts.
* **Modern Web Delivery:** Basic understanding of modern web rendering (headless CMS, React-based web applications) to enable seamless design-to-development handoffs.

**Common Hurdles & Transition Struggles:**

* **Shifting from "How It Looks" to "How It Works":** Traditional web designers often focus on surface aesthetics, typography, and banners; modern UX/Product design requires rigorous defense of user workflows, error states, edge cases, and accessibility constraints.
* **Portfolio Presentation Bar:** For designers, resumes merely secure a look at the portfolio. The biggest hurdle is building 2–3 detailed, interactive case studies that show the messy process of problem discovery, user research, wireframing iterations, and validated business impact, rather than just final polished screens.
* **Navigating the Death of Legacy CMS Web Design:** Moving past theme-based WordPress/Drupal page builders to design-system-driven web applications requires understanding how modular frontends consume design assets in production.

## 4. Mentor Discovery Strategy

The candidate should seek mentors who have navigated the evolution from traditional web and visual design into modern product and UX design:

* **Target Profiles & Job Titles:**
* **Senior Product Designer / Lead UX Designer (Travel / E-Commerce Tech):** A designer working at companies like Airbnb, Booking.com, or Hopper who can review portfolio case studies, critique interaction flows, and share modern design sprint practices.
* **Design Systems Lead:** A specialist who builds and scales multi-brand component libraries in Figma, providing concrete guidance on design tokens, auto-layout architecture, and developer handoff.
* **Staff Growth / Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Designer:** A mentor focused on landing page optimization, A/B testing, and user funnel analytics who can teach the candidate how to tie visual designs directly to customer retention and revenue metrics.



**Relevance:** These mentors will help the candidate modernize her tooling, eliminate outdated design paradigms from her resume, and craft a compelling portfolio that translates her past travel industry experience into high-value UX and product design competencies.
