# Baseline Trajectory Report

**Background:** Taylor Foster is a seasoned Senior Azure DevOps Engineer with expertise in cloud native technologies, infrastructure automation, and DevOps best practices, having delivered significant improvements in deployment times and cost reductions for multiple clients.
**Goal:** Taylor aims to continue leading and mentoring teams in adopting advanced cloud and DevOps practices to drive business objectives and technological innovation.

---

## 1. Recommended Path

* **Phase 1: Transition into Platform Engineering & GitOps Architect (Months 1–12)**
* **Objective:** Expand from client-based consulting delivery and standard Azure DevOps pipelines into building centralized Internal Developer Platforms (IDPs).
* **Focus Areas:** Advanced Kubernetes governance (AKS/EKS), GitOps architecture (ArgoCD, Flux), Infrastructure as Code module development (Terraform/Terragrunt at scale), and automated platform governance (Open Policy Agent/Kyverno).
* **Milestone:** Design and roll out a self-service developer platform using Backstage or custom CLI tools, reducing developer onboarding time and automating standardized multi-tenant cluster provisioning.


* **Phase 2: Formalize Technical Lead / Staff Platform Engineer Scope (Months 12–24)**
* **Objective:** Elevate current coaching and mentoring into formal technical leadership and enterprise architecture governance.
* **Focus Areas:** Cross-functional engineering enablement, architectural RFCs, establishing DORA metric benchmarks (deployment frequency, lead time, MTTR, change failure rate), and designing enterprise FinOps practices.
* **Milestone:** Step into a Lead DevOps / Staff Platform Engineer role, leading a squad of 4–6 DevOps/platform engineers and establishing platform standards across multiple engineering business units.


* **Phase 3: Step into Engineering Manager / Director of Cloud Infrastructure (Year 2+)**
* **Objective:** Achieve the aspirational goal of organizational leadership and continuous team mentorship driving top-level business strategy.
* **Focus Areas:** People management, engineering talent acquisition, vendor contract negotiations, enterprise cloud strategy (multi-cloud/hybrid governance), and executive stakeholder alignment.
* **Milestone:** Formally lead a department of Cloud, DevOps, and Platform Engineers as an Engineering Manager or Director of Cloud Infrastructure, managing operational budgets and driving organization-wide DevOps maturity.



**Reasoning:** The candidate already has a solid Senior DevOps baseline with proven technical skills across Azure DevOps, GitLab, Terraform, Kubernetes, and monitoring (Prometheus/Grafana), along with tangible delivery impact across consulting and enterprise healthcare environments. Their career goal explicitly emphasizes leadership, team mentorship, and driving business objectives. Rather than staying in individual-contributor project cycles, the candidate is well positioned to move through Platform Lead into formal Engineering Management, where organizational enablement and team leadership are primary deliverables.

## 2. Risk Analysis & Feasibility Flags

* **Tenure Continuity & Recent History Gap:** The documented experience ends in 2022 at ABC Consulting. In a rapidly changing cloud landscape, an unexplained multi-year gap creates automated screening risk; the candidate must immediately account for recent projects, active roles, or advisory work.
* **Consulting Delivery vs. Long-Term Platform Ownership:** Much of the senior experience was acquired at ABC Consulting across multiple short-to-medium client engagements. Enterprise leadership roles often require proof of owning long-term architectural maintenance, running multi-year platform roadmaps, and managing on-call production incidents over time.
* **Placeholder Profile Artifacts:** The resume contains placeholder text (`johndoe@email.com`, "Powered by Enhancv") and generic award listings ("Employee of the Month"). These oversights undermine executive readiness and will cause immediate disqualification by senior executive search recruiters.
* **Lack of Formal Management Scope:** While the candidate demonstrates strengths in mentoring, coaching, and agile collaboration, there is no documented record of formal people management (e.g., performance appraisals, headcount planning, compensation reviews, or budget management).

## 3. Experience Analysis & Skills to Acquire

**Technical Skills to Acquire:**

* **Internal Developer Platforms (IDPs):** Backstage, Port, or Humanitec to build self-service developer abstractions that reduce cognitive load for application developers.
* **GitOps & Advanced Kubernetes Delivery:** ArgoCD or Flux for declarative continuous deployment, progressive delivery controllers (Argo Rollouts, Flagger), and service mesh implementations (Istio/Linkerd).
* **Enterprise Policy as Code & Security:** Automating security shift-left practices using Kyverno, Open Policy Agent (Gatekeeper), Trivy/Snyk container scanning, and HashiCorp Vault enterprise integration.
* **Cloud FinOps & Cost Governance:** Deep cost observability and automated allocation tools (Kubecost, Azure Cost Management APIs) to directly connect infrastructure architecture to executive business margins.

**Common Hurdles & Transition Struggles:**

* **Shifting from Client Delivery to Organizational Change:** In consulting, engineers solve predefined client problems and move on; leading DevOps transformations within a single enterprise requires continuous political navigation, overcoming developer resistance, and driving cultural change.
* **Stepping Away from Tactical Implementation:** Senior engineers who transition into leadership often struggle to stop writing Terraform code or building pipelines themselves, rather than focusing on unblocking their teams and establishing engineering guidelines.
* **Connecting Technical Metrics to Business Value:** Bridging the language gap between DevOps technical achievements (e.g., pipeline speed, container density) and executive business outcomes (e.g., customer churn reduction, feature time-to-revenue, cloud gross margin improvement).

## 4. Mentor Discovery Strategy

The candidate should seek mentors who have navigated the trajectory from senior hands-on cloud engineering to executive infrastructure and platform leadership:

* **Target Profiles & Job Titles:**
* **Director / VP of Platform Engineering:** An executive who has successfully built and scaled platform engineering and DevOps teams from the ground up within a mid-to-large product company.
* **Head of Cloud Architecture / Cloud Center of Excellence (CCoE) Lead:** A leader who specializes in large-scale cloud governance, enterprise adoption frameworks, and guiding business units through modern DevOps maturity.
* **Engineering Manager (Ex-Senior DevOps/SRE):** A people leader who recently navigated the transition from senior technical contributor to engineering manager, offering tactical coaching on performance management, delegation, and executive communication.



**Relevance:** These mentors will help the candidate bridge the gap between technical consulting delivery and long-term organizational leadership. They can provide concrete guidance on packaging their mentoring strengths into formal leadership credentials and structuring an executive-level career narrative centered on business transformation.
