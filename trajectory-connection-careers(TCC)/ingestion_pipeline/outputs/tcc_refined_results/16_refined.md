# Career Transition Guide: Senior DevOps Engineer to DevOps Engineer

## Phase 1: Core Upskilling

To successfully transition from a Senior DevOps Engineer to a DevOps Engineer, you must focus on acquiring and consolidating key technical skills. The primary hurdles you will face include navigating role ambiguity and difficulty in consolidating technical skills despite repeated use. Our graph data reveals that 10 professionals in our dataset who made this exact jump explicitly struggled with consolidating technical skills, indicating a need for targeted practice and reinforcement.

### Action Steps:
1. **Skill Reinforcement**: Identify and prioritize the technical skills that are critical for a DevOps Engineer role. Focus on areas such as cloud technologies, automation tools, and continuous integration/continuous deployment (CI/CD) practices. Engage in hands-on projects and real-world applications to solidify your understanding and proficiency.
2. **Role Ambiguity Management**: Develop a clear understanding of the responsibilities and expectations of a DevOps Engineer. Engage with industry professionals and mentors to gain insights into the role's nuances and best practices. This will help you navigate any ambiguity and ensure you are well-prepared for the transition.

## Phase 2: Transition

Once you have strengthened your technical skills and clarified your role expectations, the next phase involves making the actual transition. This phase is crucial for bridging the gap between your current role and the target role.

### Action Steps:
1. **Leadership Experience**: Gain experience in mentoring and guiding junior developers. This will help you build the necessary leadership skills and prepare you for the responsibilities of a Technical Lead. Focus on developing your communication, collaboration, and problem-solving skills.
2. **Cloud-Based Practices**: Leverage your existing knowledge of cloud technologies to enhance your DevOps practices. Engage with cloud-based DevOps tools and platforms to stay current with industry trends and best practices.
