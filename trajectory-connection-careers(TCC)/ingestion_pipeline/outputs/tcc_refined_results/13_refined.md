# Career Transition Guide: Senior Azure DevOps Engineer to DevOps Engineer

## Phase 1: Core Upskilling

To successfully transition from a Senior Azure DevOps Engineer to a DevOps Engineer, you must focus on building a robust foundation of technical expertise and leadership skills. The Realist's path, which includes a step from Developer to Senior Developer before moving to Team Lead, provides a more pragmatic approach. This intermediate step allows you to accumulate necessary experience and skills, thereby mitigating the risk of credential gaps and skill deficiencies.

### Key Steps:
1. **Enhance Technical Proficiency**: Focus on acquiring and consolidating technical skills that are essential for a DevOps Engineer role. This includes deepening your understanding of cloud platforms, automation tools, and continuous integration/continuous deployment (CI/CD) practices.
2. **Leadership Development**: Begin to develop leadership skills by taking on more responsibilities within your current role. This could involve leading small projects, mentoring junior team members, and participating in cross-functional teams.

## Phase 2: Transition

Once you have built a strong foundation of technical and leadership skills, you can start preparing for the transition to a DevOps Engineer role.

### Key Steps:
1. **Bridge Role Ambiguity**: Address the common hurdle of navigating role ambiguity by clearly defining your career goals and aligning them with the responsibilities of a DevOps Engineer. This involves understanding the role's technical and non-technical requirements and preparing accordingly.
2. **Consolidate Technical Skills**: Despite repeated use, many professionals struggle to consolidate their technical skills effectively. To overcome this, focus on continuous learning and practical application of your skills. Engage in hands-on projects, contribute to open-source initiatives, and seek out opportunities to work on complex technical challenges.
