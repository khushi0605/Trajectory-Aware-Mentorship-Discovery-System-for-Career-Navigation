# Career Transition Guide: Oracle PL/SQL Developer to Software Engineer

## Phase 1: Core Upskilling

To successfully transition from an Oracle PL/SQL Developer to a Software Engineer, you must focus on acquiring the necessary skills and experience that align with the recommended path of Developer to Senior Developer to Technical Lead. This structured approach ensures a balanced and feasible progression, mitigating potential skill gaps and credential concerns.

### Key Skills to Acquire

1. **Technical Leadership Skills**: Transitioning from a Developer to a Technical Lead requires a strong foundation in technical leadership. You must develop the ability to mentor junior developers, manage project timelines, and oversee technical decision-making processes. This will prepare you for the responsibilities and challenges of a Technical Lead role.

2. **Advanced Programming Skills**: Enhance your programming skills by focusing on languages and frameworks commonly used in software engineering roles. This includes learning Python, JavaScript, and other relevant technologies. Additionally, ensure you consolidate your technical skills through repeated use and application in real-world projects.

## Phase 2: Transition

Once you have acquired the necessary skills and experience, the next phase involves transitioning into a Senior Developer role and then to a Technical Lead. This phase requires strategic planning and execution to ensure a smooth and successful career progression.

### Strategic Steps

1. **Seek Mentorship**: Identify and connect with experienced professionals who have successfully navigated similar career transitions. Their guidance and insights will be invaluable in helping you navigate the challenges and opportunities of your transition.

2. **Build a Strong Portfolio**: Develop a portfolio of projects that showcase your technical skills and leadership capabilities. This will be crucial when applying for Senior Developer and Technical Lead roles.

3. **Network and Collaborate**: Engage with industry professionals through networking events, conferences, and online communities. Building a strong professional network will provide you with additional support and opportunities for career advancement.
