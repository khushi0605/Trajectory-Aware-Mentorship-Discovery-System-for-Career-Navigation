## Phase 2: Transition

Once you have acquired the necessary skills, you should focus on gaining practical experience and building a strong professional network. This phase involves transitioning from a Developer to a Senior Developer and then to a Technical Lead.

### Overcoming Transition Themes

1. **The Infrastructure Gap**: Many professionals struggle with the transition due to a lack of hands-on experience with critical infrastructure tools and technologies. To overcome this, you must actively seek out projects and opportunities that allow you to work with Linux, networking, and security tools. Engage in open-source projects, internships, or part-time roles that provide real-world experience.

2. **The Skill Mismatch**: The transition often requires a significant shift in skill sets, particularly in areas like JavaScript, React, and GitHub. To bridge this gap, you should enroll in relevant courses, attend workshops, and participate in coding challenges. Focus on building a portfolio of projects that demonstrate your proficiency in these areas.

3. **The Leadership Challenge**: Moving from a Developer to a Technical Lead requires strong leadership and management skills. You must take on leadership roles within your current organization or seek out opportunities in startups or smaller companies where you can gain experience in team management and project leadership.
